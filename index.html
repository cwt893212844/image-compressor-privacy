Privacy Policy — Image Compressor

Effective date: 2026-09-20
App: Image Compressor (com.lowkeyworks.imagecompressor)
Version: 1.0.0

1. Introduction

Image Compressor is an Android utility that compresses images on your device. This policy describes what the app accesses and how your data is handled. No account is required. The app does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Photos you select

The app accesses only images you choose through the Android system Photo Picker. Selected images are read to decode, compress, and re-encode them locally.

3. Metadata (EXIF)

Source images may contain EXIF metadata, including camera information and GPS location when present. By default, metadata is removed. If you enable Keep Metadata, standard EXIF (including GPS when present) may be preserved in the output.

4. On-device processing

Compression runs on your device. The app declares no INTERNET permission and does not upload your images to our servers.

5. Temporary files

Temporary app-private files may be used during processing, saving, or sharing and are cleaned according to the app lifecycle.

6. Saved outputs

When you save, results are written to your gallery under Pictures/ImageCompressor/. On Android 8–9, storage write permission may be requested for gallery save.

7. Sharing

Sharing uses Android’s system share sheet. You choose the receiving app. That app’s privacy policy applies after you share.

8. What we do not collect

  • No accounts or login
  • No analytics or crash SDKs in this version
  • No ads or advertising identifiers
  • No location permission
  • No server upload of your photos by the app

9. App settings

Default compression preferences are stored locally on your device (DataStore).

10. Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be revised.

12. Contact

For privacy questions about Image Compressor (com.lowkeyworks.imagecompressor), open an issue at: https://github.com/cwt893212844/image-compressor-privacy/issues.
